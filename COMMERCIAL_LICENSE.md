## What a commercial license is

A commercial license is a **separate proprietary license** from the copyright
holder, offered as an alternative to the AGPL **for the licensee named in the
agreement**. It does not re-license the public repository or remove the AGPL
for anyone else.

Typical commercial terms (negotiated in the signed agreement) include:

1. **Proprietary use.** Permission to use, modify, and deploy SSOG in

2. **Private modifications.** No requirement to publish your internal forks or

3. **Support (if purchased).** Technical support and implementation consulting

## How to request a license

Email **raphael.pisoni+ssog@gmail.com** with subject:

`Commercial License Inquiry - SSOG`

Include a short description of the product or service, how SSOG would be used
(embedded library, training pipeline, hosted API, etc.), and your
organization's scale (stage, headcount, or revenue band is enough). That is
enough to send an accurate quote and draft.
